# Streamstiny

A native Destiny.gg chat and video app for iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

## Install

Build **Streamstiny.tipa** using the [local build instructions](docs/building.md), then install it with TrollStore on a compatible device. This repository contains source code; release packages and tags were not imported.

## Watch and chat

- Tap the video to show controls. Use the stream menu to switch streams or **+ Add stream** to paste a URL.
- YouTube livestreams use the native player. Paste a video link, a channel live link, or an `@handle` URL.
- Twitch channels, VODs, and clips also use native playback, with quality selection from the available stream variants.
- Hold the video for 2× playback. In landscape, double-tap to show or hide chat.
- Open chat settings for **Playback**, **Chat**, **Appearance**, and **Account**.
- In **Playback**, enable automatic catch-up and choose a target buffer in seconds, start at the highest quality, or enable either viewer counter: DGG viewers and overall stream viewers. Both counts come from Destiny.gg.
- In **Chat → Layout**, choose a landscape overlay and adjust landscape chat size. Portrait chat fills the space below the video. **Chat → Messages** controls text size and timestamps.
- Sign in to Destiny.gg under **Accounts**, alongside Twitch. Tap pins and polls to expand them and vote; use chat tools for users, whispers, and search. Long press messages for user actions and emotes in the picker to favorite them. Type `/help` for commands.
- Use **Account → Twitch** to optionally sign in for eligible account benefits or refresh Twitch authorization.
- Picture in Picture starts automatically when you leave the app, when supported and enabled on your device.

Stream availability and quality depend on the provider and your device. Twitch may require authorization; protected videos may be unavailable. Viewer counts appear when Destiny.gg supplies them.
